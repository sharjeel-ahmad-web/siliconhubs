# About Section Images

## Purpose
Images for the about section showcasing company, office, or team at work.

## Image Requirements
- **Dimensions:** 1200x800px (3:2 ratio)
- **Format:** JPG or WebP
- **Max Size:** 400KB
- **Style:** Professional, authentic, representative of company culture
- **Content:** Office space, team working, company events, etc.

## Naming Convention
- `office-space.jpg`
- `team-working.jpg`
- `company-culture.jpg`
- `about-hero.jpg`

## How to Add Images

1. **Optimize your image** (< 400KB)
2. **Upload to this folder**
3. **Update the about section component:**
   - Open `components/ui/about-section.tsx`
   - Update image source in the component
   - Update alt text for accessibility

## Image Content Ideas
- Office environment
- Team collaboration
- Company events
- Work culture moments
- Product/service in action
- Client meetings (with permission)

## Photography Tips
- Natural lighting works best
- Show authentic moments
- Include diversity
- Avoid posed, stiff photos
- Capture company personality
- Ensure all people have signed releases

## Alternative Content
If photos aren't available:
- Abstract illustrations
- Brand-related graphics
- Icon-based designs
- Animated graphics
