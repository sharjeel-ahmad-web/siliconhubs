# SiliconHubs — Agency Knowledge Base

## Who We Are

SiliconHubs is a premium digital solutions agency helping businesses grow online with
automation, AI, and modern web experiences. We serve startups, SMEs, and established brands.

## Services (with short descriptions)

- **N8N Automations** — Workflow automation connecting your apps (ChatGPT, Google Sheets,
  Slack, CRM, e-mail) to save hours every week. Friction-free integrations, no-code builds,
  and human-in-the-loop automations.
- **Chatbot Development** — AI chatbots for websites and WhatsApp, trained on your business
  data, with human hand-off when needed. 24/7 lead capture.
- **Web Design & Development** — High-performance, conversion-focused websites (Next.js,
  React, WordPress, Shopify) with modern animations and SEO built in.
- **WordPress Development** — Custom themes, plugins, WooCommerce stores, and performance
  optimization for WordPress sites.
- **Shopify Development** — Custom Shopify stores, theme tweaks, app integrations, and CRO
  improvements for e-commerce.
- **SEO Services** — On-page, technical SEO, content strategy, Google Search Console setup,
  analytics dashboards, and conversion tracking.
- **Digital Marketing** — Social media management, paid ads support, email marketing, and
  funnel optimization.

## Typical Process

1. Free consultation call to understand goals.
2. Proposal with scope, timeline, and fixed pricing.
3. Design & development sprints with regular updates.
4. QA, launch, and post-launch support.
